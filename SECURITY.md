# Security policy

We take the security of moonbase and of the apps our customers run on it seriously. Thank you for helping keep them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a security problem.**

Report it privately in one of these ways:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting (preferred for anything in these repositories).** Open the affected repository, go to the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Only the moonbase maintainers can see the report.
2. **Email [admin@moonbase.cloud](mailto:admin@moonbase.cloud).** Use this for the moonbase platform itself (moonbase.cloud, app.moonbase.cloud, hosted apps, the connector at `https://app.moonbase.cloud/mcp`). This is the security contact published on [moonbase.cloud/security](https://moonbase.cloud/security) and in [security.txt](https://moonbase.cloud/.well-known/security.txt). That page also has a report form.

Please include:

- what is affected (URL, repository, file or connector tool);
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- the impact you think it has;
- whether you want to be credited.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 2 business days and keep you updated while we work on it.
- We tell you when a fix is out, and credit you if you want that.
- The full disclosure process is described on [moonbase.cloud/security](https://moonbase.cloud/security).
- We do not run a paid bug bounty program.

## Good-faith research

Please:

- test only against your own account and your own projects;
- do not access, change or delete other people's data;
- do not run automated scanning that degrades the service for other users;
- do not use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks;
- give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you share details publicly.

## Scope

In scope: the moonbase platform and all public repositories in this organization, including [moonbase-skill](https://github.com/Moonbasecloud/moonbase-skill), [moonbase-examples](https://github.com/Moonbasecloud/moonbase-examples) and [moonbase-deploy-action](https://github.com/Moonbasecloud/moonbase-deploy-action).

Abuse on a moonbase-hosted app (phishing, malware, spam) is not a vulnerability report. Use the abuse form on [moonbase.cloud/security](https://moonbase.cloud/security) instead.
